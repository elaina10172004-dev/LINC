## Benchmark Data

The repository includes generated TSP/CVRP/CVRPTW evaluation sets and converted
benchmark subsets. TSPLIB, CVRPLIB/XML, Solomon, and Homberger data should be
cited according to their original benchmark terms when used in publications.
